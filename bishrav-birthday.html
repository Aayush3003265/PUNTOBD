A letter for you

BABY,

Six years ago, I never imagined that one person could become such an important part of my life. You have brought so much happiness into my world in ways words can never fully explain. You have a beautiful way of turning ordinary days into unforgettable memories, simply by being there. Your kindness, your patience, and the way you always care for me even in the smallest moments are gifts I treasure every single day.

One of the things I admire most about you is how you always seem to understand me without needing many words. You notice when I'm not myself, you make me smile when I need it most, and you remind me what it feels like to be truly loved. Your love has never needed grand gestures because it has always been genuine, steady, and unconditional. That quiet, constant love is one of the greatest blessings in my life.

I may not always express how deeply grateful I am for you, but today I wanted to do something that reflects even a small part of what you mean to me. This small gesture is my way of showing my appreciation for you for every smile, every laugh, every comforting hug, every conversation, and every beautiful memory we've created together.

Happy Birthday, my love. I hope this new year brings you as much happiness as you've brought into my life. May your beautiful smile never fade, your heart always stay as kind as it is today, and may we continue creating countless memories together. No matter where life takes us, I will always be thankful that six years ago, our paths crossed.

Happy birthday. I love you — today, and every ordinary day after it.

To many, many more.

From year one to year six, and every year still to come — happy birthday, BABY. This one's just getting started.
